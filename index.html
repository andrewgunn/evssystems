What we do

Everything to keep your home safe and connected

Burglar alarms

Wired and wireless alarms from Texecom and Pyronix, with app control so you can arm, disarm and get alerts wherever you are. Dusk-to-dawn lit siren boxes put off intruders before they try.

CCTV

Sharp HD Hikvision cameras with night vision, recording to a secure recorder at home. Watch live or play back footage on your phone at any time.

Smart home

Control your heating, alarm, cameras and gates remotely from an app. Check in while you're away and come home to a warm house.

Electric gates

Automation for new or existing gates using Nice motors, with remote fobs, keypads or phone control. We also repair gates and replace motors.

Door entry & access

Intercoms and keyless entry by tag, code or smartphone, from a single front door to a whole block of flats.

TV, Wi-Fi & AV

Wall-mounted TVs with hidden cables, Sky and aerial points, sound systems and Wi-Fi that reaches every room.
